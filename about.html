About The Platform And How It Works

Our Story And Safety

Built by students, for students. We created a single, trustworthy spot for campus lost and found so you don't have to wade through chaotic group chats.

Only logged-in university students can post reports or contact item owners. Zero outside spammers or scammers allowed.

Always meet up in well-lit, high-traffic campus locations like the Library , Student Centre, or Campus Security.

Keep personal info safe! Never upload images showing full student ID numbers, phone numbers, or residential addresses

How It Works

Step 1: Drop the Deets

Found something on a bench or lost your airpods near the lecture hall? Fill out a 1-minute report with a quick photo, description, and exact campus location.

Step 2: Spot Your Match

Browse through the live listings using easy filters like Electronics, Keys, ID Cards, or Water Bottles to quickly find what you're looking for.

Step 3: Safe Hand-Off

Connect directly via verified campus email and pick a safe public campus spot to hand over the item and celebrate a successful reunion!
